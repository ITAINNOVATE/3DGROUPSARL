Who are we?

3D-GROUPE SARL is a company specializing in the design, structuring, and implementation of high value-added agro-pasto-forestry projects.

We support investors and agro-business enthusiasts in the development of profitable, sustainable, and legally secure projects, thanks to full mastery of the value chain.

Our approach is based on the identification and securing of land, technical and financial structuring, as well as the final valuation of resources.

With deep local expertise, we transform your investment ideas into concrete assets that carry a lasting economic, social, and environmental impact in Benin.

Our Vision

To become the West African reference for sustainable agro-forestry development, reconciling performance and ecology.

Our Mission

To transform land opportunities into productive assets secured by cutting-edge technical and strategic expertise.
