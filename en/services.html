Our Services

Complete expertise to secure and make your
agro-forestry investments profitable.

At 3D-GROUPE SARL, we provide you with complete expertise to design, secure and implement high-performance and sustainable agro-pasto-forestry projects. We operate in:

1- Project design, planning and drafting

  • Development of customized agro-pasto-forestry projects.
  • Technical, economic and environmental studies.
  • Business plans, financial engineering and bankable files.
  • Administrative support and security of procedures.

2- Land: acquisition, leasing or securitization of land capital

  • Identification of land adapted to projects (favorable agro-ecological zones).
  • Negotiation and facilitation of acquisition or lease procedures.
  • Legal, social and environmental compliance.

3- Establishment and management of forest plantations

  • Site preparation and installation of plantations (teak, Gmelina, cashew, etc.).
  • Maintenance, technical follow-up and continuous monitoring.
  • Sustainable management and yield optimization until maturity (5, 10 years and up).

4- Forest logging and valuation

  • Organization and supervision of logging operations.
  • Certification and traceability of wood according to current standards.
  • Primary processing with our partners (sawmills).
  • Possibility of exporting or importing forest products according to client needs.

5- Support for processing companies

  • Support for installation and administrative compliance.
  • Provision of private and certified forest resources.
  • Structuring reliable supply chains.
  • Technical and strategic support for a secure start-up.

Methodology

01

Project Analysis

Active listening to your needs, preliminary feasibility study and definition of profitability and impact objectives.

02

Land Securing

Identification of land, legal verification, and formalization of ownership or lease acts.

03

Technical & Financial Structuring

Carrying out detailed technical studies, business plan, and operational planning.

04

Field Implementation

Soil preparation, acquisition of certified inputs/seedlings, planting and installation of infrastructure.

05

Sustainable Follow-up & Management

Regular monitoring, technical maintenance, and periodic progress reports.

Valuation & Impact

Harvesting, processing, marketing/export and measurement of socio-environmental impact.
