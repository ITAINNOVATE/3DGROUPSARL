Our Methodological Framework

From strategic land feasibility to global timber export: a proven, end-to-end framework turning agricultural vision into high-yield, sustainable assets.

Technical Rigor & Legal Assurance

Structured Execution Across 6 Key Milestones

While every agroforestry venture is unique, long-term success requires uncompromising operational excellence. 3D-GROUPE SARL deploys an integrated approach securing maximum financial yield, tenure legitimacy, and ecosystem preservation.

01 🔍

Diagnostic & Feasibility Studies

Comprehensive soil sampling, hydrology assessment, local microclimate profiling and socio-economic analysis. Establishing precise technical specifications and target yield models.

Key Deliverables

Agro-ecological study report & investment opportunity memorandum.

02 📜

Land Securitization & Legal Engineering

Identification of high-potential arable tracts, public notices, georeferenced cadastral boundary surveys, and securing formal legal ownership or long-term emphyteutic leases.

Key Deliverables

Clean tenure portfolio & certified cadastral boundary survey.

03 📊

Technical Project Design & Financial Modeling

Detailed architectural planting design, 5-to-25-year operational expenditure schedules, and bankable investment proposals for institutional funds and private partners.

Key Deliverables

Bankable Business Plan & multi-year cash flow projections.

04 🌱

Field Development & Planting Operations

Mechanized or manual land clearing, supply of certified vigorous saplings (Teak, Gmelina, Cashew, etc.), precise grid staking, pitting, and planting following strict silvicultural standards.

Key Deliverables

Developed plantation acreage, guaranteed recovery rate > 95%.

05 🛡️

Silvicultural Management & Stewardship

Scheduled maintenance, firebreak clearance, selective pruning, commercial thinning, and drone-assisted aerial growth monitoring with transparent quarterly reporting.

Key Deliverables

Bi-annual timber inventory audits & dynamic GIS growth mapping.

06 🚢

Harvesting, Value Addition & Export

Sustainable felling plans, log cubage, local sawmill processing, and direct connection with vetted international off-takers for streamlined export without intermediaries.

Key Deliverables

Firm off-take purchase agreements & certified traceability.
